Petra

Petra is a famous historical city in, Jordan. It was built by the Nabataeans more than 2,000 years ago . Petra is known for its beautiful buildings carved into pink rocks . It is one of the New Seven Wonders of the World. Every year, many tourists from different countries visit Petra to enjoy its beauty and learn about its history.
